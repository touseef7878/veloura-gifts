# The Veloura — Design Direction

## Reference Ground Truth

The Instagram profile **@the_veloura77 / @VeLoura** is the source of truth for the business tone: “Wrapped with love, delivered with care,” with a promise of customized, premium, memorable gifts for birthdays, anniversaries, and more. The profile shows a refined cream, teal, and champagne identity with an artisanal wrapping and celebration focus, and uses Instagram direct messages as the primary ordering path.

## Chosen Approach: Quiet-Luxury Gift Atelier

### Design Movement

Contemporary editorial luxury, borrowing from boutique packaging studios, independent fashion lookbooks, and tactile stationery brands rather than conventional e-commerce grids.

### Core Principles

1. **Thoughtful over loud:** Let texture, whitespace, and material cues communicate premium quality.
2. **Asymmetric rhythm:** Use editorial offsets, cropped imagery, and generous margins to avoid a template-like storefront.
3. **Humanized luxury:** Pair refined typography with warm microcopy so the brand feels personal, not distant.
4. **Clear next step:** Every major section gently resolves into Instagram or WhatsApp contact.

### Color Philosophy

Warm ivory creates the feeling of unwrapping tissue paper; deep teal gives the brand its ownable signature and echoes the existing profile mark; champagne gold adds a quiet sense of occasion; muted blush softens celebrations without becoming overly sweet. Contrast remains high enough for comfortable reading.

### Layout Paradigm

A one-page editorial story: a narrow announcement line, an airy split hero, a staggered collection strip, a tactile process section, and a closing contact invitation. The page should feel like a lookbook that happens to make ordering easy.

### Signature Elements

- A ribbon-loop mark used as a visible brand stamp, favicon, and section motif.
- Hairline champagne rules and small uppercase labels that feel like packaging annotations.
- Oversized italic serif phrases used sparingly as emotional punctuation.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should feel like handling a beautifully wrapped object: buttons press with a small physical response, images lift slightly on hover, and navigation stays direct and calm. No noisy carousels or hidden e-commerce behavior; contact actions open the customer’s preferred channel.

### Animation

Use short, soft reveal transitions for hero copy and collection cards, with staggered timing and transform/opacity only. Image hover states should be a restrained scale and slight brightness lift. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion` for all non-essential movement.

### Typography System

Use **Cormorant Garamond** for display headlines, italic emphasis, and the wordmark-like “VeLoura” treatment. Use **Manrope** for navigation, body copy, labels, and calls to action. Headlines should be high contrast and editorial; supporting copy should be comfortably spaced and never too small.

### Brand Essence

**The Veloura turns everyday occasions into beautifully wrapped memories for people who want their gifts to feel considered, personal, and unforgettable.** Personality: **warm, discerning, thoughtful.**

### Brand Voice

Headlines should feel intimate and confident. CTAs should sound like an invitation to co-create, not a hard sell. Microcopy should be specific and reassuring.

Example lines:

> “Make the moment look like them.”

> “Tell us who it’s for. We’ll make it unforgettable.”

### Wordmark & Logo

Use a distinctive ribbon-loop symbol as the visual mark, paired with a high-contrast serif treatment of “VeLoura” and a small uppercase descriptor. The mark should be bold enough to remain recognizable as a favicon and header icon.

### Signature Brand Color

**Veloura Teal — #0D4D4A.** It is deep, calm, and distinctive against warm neutrals, giving the brand a signature beyond generic blush-and-gold gifting.

## Style Decisions

- The ribbon-loop symbol is the primary brand stamp across the header, hero badge, section marker, closing invitation, footer, and favicon.
- Supporting imagery follows one tactile editorial direction: warm ivory paper, champagne ribbon, soft florals, and occasional Veloura Teal accents.
- Long ivory sections use packaging-inspired structure — section numbering, hairline rules, small uppercase annotations, and offset compositions — so whitespace feels intentional.
